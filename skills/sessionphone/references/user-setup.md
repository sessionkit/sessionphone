# user-setup: what the person does, in their words

Read this to them, or hand it over. Two minutes, phone only.

1. **Install Delta Chat** from the App Store or Google Play. It's free, and
   there's no sign-up with an email company: the app makes you an account.
2. **Open it and tap "Create new profile".** Type your name. That's the
   whole account. If it asks for a server, leave the default.
3. **Scan the code.** Tap the QR icon at the top right, and point the phone
   at the page your assistant opened on the screen.
4. **Wait a moment.** Your assistant sends "Linked" when it's done. From
   then on you can text it, and you're the one person who can ring it.

Messages are end-to-end encrypted, and the code you scanned is what made
that possible. If you get a new phone, scan again.

## If you already use Delta Chat

Skip 1 and 2. Scan from your existing profile.

## Words to avoid when explaining it

- **"email":** it uses email underneath, but calling it email invites the
  wrong expectations. It's a messenger.
- **"server", "relay", "chatmail":** they never need a name for any of it.
